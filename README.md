# MB Document Scanner

Turn paper into clean, shareable documents from your iPhone or iPad. Scan pages, bring in existing photos or PDFs, review the results, and export a polished PDF or JPEG—all with your documents kept on your device.

## Try the beta

[Join MB Document Scanner on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/9B76DMGY)

Available for iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.

## What you can do

- **Scan or import pages.** Capture a multi-page document with automatic edge correction, choose photos, import pages from a PDF, or paste an image.
- **Keep documents organized.** Save scans in an on-device library, add pages later, and reorder pages to get the document in shape.
- **Review text and page quality.** On-device text recognition lets you select and copy detected text. Readability, sharpness, contrast, and image-detail checks can flag pages that may need another look.
- **Edit pages.** Crop, rotate, add text or freehand markup, and permanently redact sensitive details.
- **Share the way you need.** Export multi-page PDFs or individual JPEGs, with a choice of compression quality.

Quality checks are helpful review hints, not a guarantee that every word or issue has been detected. Give important pages a visual check before sharing.

## Your documents stay yours

Scans and text recognition stay on your device. The app needs no account or network connection, and has no ads or analytics. Nothing is shared until you choose to export it.
